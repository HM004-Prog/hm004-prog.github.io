THE QUESTION BEHIND MY WORK

What connects a material’s
structure to its function?

I am a materials science researcher with a background in physics. My doctoral research at Université Paris-Saclay explores the design and development of functional materials for energy and environmental applications.

I am interested in understanding how composition and processing influence a material’s properties, and using these relationships to guide the development of materials for practical applications.

HOW I WORK

Understand.

Experimental expertise across materials synthesis, characterization, and performance evaluation.

I

Materials synthesis & processing

  • Solid-state synthesis
  • Solvothermal and hydrothermal synthesis
  • Sol–gel processing
  • Biomass-assisted synthesis
  • Physical vapour deposition by magnetron sputtering
  • High-energy ball milling
II

Structural & surface characterization

  • Powder X-ray diffraction
  • Rietveld refinement and Le Bail whole-pattern fitting
  • Scanning electron microscopy
  • Transmission electron microscopy
  • Atomic force microscopy
  • X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy
  • Nitrogen physisorption and Brunauer–Emmett–Teller surface-area analysis
III

Spectroscopy & electrochemical measurements

  • Raman spectroscopy
  • Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy
  • Ultraviolet–visible diffuse reflectance spectroscopy
  • Photoluminescence spectroscopy
  • Cyclic voltammetry
  • Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy
  • Evaluation of oxygen and hydrogen evolution reactions
  • Photoelectrochemical measurements
